## Company Q3 2026 priorities
*Source: Confluence page 41002 (TW-PRIO-Q3) · Owner: Rosalind K. (CEO) · Last updated 2026-06-22*

- **T1 — Get clinics paid faster:** cut the average clinic's days-to-collect from 31 to under 20.

## Ledger team — Q3 2026
*Source: Confluence page 41108 (LEDG-OKR-Q3) · Owner: Amara O. · Last updated 2026-07-01*
*Commitment: KRs are committed unless marked (aspirational).*

### Objective L1: Clinics get paid without chasing anyone
- **KR L1.1:** Reduce median days-to-collect from 31 to 19 across active clinics.
- **KR L1.2:** Raise auto-reconciled payment share from 62% to 85% of transactions.
- **KR L1.3:** Cut billing support tickets per 100 clinics from 14 to 8 per month.

### Objective L2: Billing stays boring as we scale
- **KR L2.1:** Hold billing run success rate at or above 99.7% through Q3.
- **KR L2.2:** Ship the new payout ledger service to production by Sep 18. *(aspirational)*

*Notes: L1.2 assumes the clinic bank-link rework lands from Onboarding in July — Nadia's team owns the connector. Days-to-collect is measured from the finance dashboard (page 41310).*
