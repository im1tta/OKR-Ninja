## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 11 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The portfolio's worst alignment risk is **AL-11 Circular dependency**: Courier instruments only after Core Systems GAs the telemetry SDK, Core Systems GAs only after Insights validates schema v3, and Insights validates only after Courier instruments — three hard edges, one loop, and no page that notices it. As written, the quarter's whole telemetry program cannot start.
On goodness, no anti-pattern repeats across teams — eight distinct AP-XX instances, one each — and the single Critical one is **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator**, Dispatch's "failure rate" KR, which names no population and therefore cannot be scored honestly at quarter end.
Two portfolio-level defects come next: the metric both Dispatch and Accounts call on-time delivery rate carries two incompatible definitions and a 13-point baseline gap across Dispatch and Accounts (AL-08 Terminology collision), and company priority C1's headline number — ARR $14M → $17.5M, due at end of Q1 — has no owning KR anywhere in the five teams (AL-10 Strategy coverage gap).
Recommended first action: Core Systems convenes Courier and Insights in week 1 and breaks the cycle by releasing the already code-complete SDK pre-GA.
