**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 13 Major, 3 Minor findings.
The portfolio's biggest threat is AL-11 Circular dependency: Courier waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, Core Systems waits on Insights' schema validation, and Insights waits on Courier's instrumentation — three teams, no valid execution order, all of it under company priority C4.

Recommended first action: a Courier/Core Systems/Insights sequencing session to break the SDK–schema–instrumentation cycle in the first two weeks of the quarter.
