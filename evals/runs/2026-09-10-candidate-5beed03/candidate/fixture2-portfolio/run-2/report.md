## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 13 Major, 3 Minor findings.
The portfolio's biggest threat is AL-11 Circular dependency: Courier waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, Core Systems waits on Insights' schema validation, and Insights waits on Courier's instrumentation — three teams, no valid execution order as written, and the whole C4 telemetry-consolidation bet sits inside the loop.
The most common quality issue is AP-01 Task Masquerading as KR (Courier and Accounts each state a coverage percentage of their own delivery as the result).
Two teams also measure "On-time delivery rate" with different formulas, populations, sources and targets (AL-08 Terminology collision) — the metric C2 reports to customers, so leadership is rolling up two numbers under one name.
Accounts marks all four objectives P0 (AP-05 Everything Is a P0), and its committed CSAT baseline of 86 contradicts the Q4 review's 78 (AL-09 Baseline disagreement).
Dispatch carries the only Critical goodness defect: a KR whose "failure rate" names no population (AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator).
Recommended first action: convene Courier, Core Systems and Insights this week to break the telemetry cycle before any of the three plans further work against it.
