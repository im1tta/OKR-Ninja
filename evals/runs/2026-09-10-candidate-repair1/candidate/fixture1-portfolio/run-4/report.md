## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 4 teams reviewed (Payments, Growth, Platform, Data) for Q3 2026; 5 Critical, 8 Major, 1 Minor findings.
The single worst alignment risk is **AL-02 Conflicting metrics / adversarial incentives**: Payments is pushing step-up verification coverage to 90% of transactions on the same checkout surface where Growth commits to a 10-point conversion lift, and neither page mentions the other team.
The most common goodness anti-pattern is **AP-04 KR Without Baseline** — three KRs across Growth and Platform state a target with no starting point, so neither ambition nor progress can be judged.
Two KRs name no instrument at all (Platform's developer-satisfaction score, Data's "data quality"), the portfolio's only Critical goodness findings, both **AP-09 Metric Nobody Can Measure**.
Company priority C4 (Brightledger Capital, due Sep 30) has zero coverage across all four teams — **AL-10 Strategy coverage gap** — and Growth's Aug 15 upgrade launch depends on a Payments API that does not reach GA until Sep 26 (**AL-06 Timeline mismatch**).
Platform declares Q3 "fully committed" while both Payments and Data plan on its capacity (**AL-07 Resource contention**), and Growth and Data hold separate targets on the same activation metric (**AL-03 Duplicated / overlapping objectives**).
Recommended first action: a joint Payments/Growth session on checkout guardrails and a named owner for C4, both inside the first two weeks of the quarter.
