# Coppervale — Q1 2027 OKR portfolio review

Mode: portfolio (5 teams in scope: Dispatch, Courier, Core Systems, Insights, Accounts). Period: Q1 2027. Strategy source: "Company Q1 2027 priorities" (Confluence page 91050), lines 10–13 of the export.

## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 9 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The portfolio's biggest threat is AL-11 Circular dependency: Courier's instrumentation waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, Core Systems' GA waits on Insights' schema validation, and Insights' validation waits on Courier's instrumentation — a three-team wait with no valid execution order as written.
The most severe quality defect is AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator on Dispatch KR DS2.4, whose "failure rate" names no population, so the KR can never be honestly scored.
No goodness anti-pattern repeats: all eight goodness findings carry distinct AP-IDs across four teams, so there is no single portfolio-wide quality pattern to fix at once.
Two metric-integrity defects sit under the company's own churn priority: "On-time delivery rate" is defined two incompatible ways by Dispatch and Accounts (AL-08 Terminology collision), and Accounts' committed CSAT target is calibrated on a baseline the Q4 business review contradicts by 8 points (AL-09 Baseline disagreement).
Core Systems' committed delivery-velocity objective claims to serve enterprise churn but measures none of the three drivers the company priority names (AL-05 Cascade drift).
Recommended first action: a Courier/Core Systems/Insights sequencing session in week 1 to break the telemetry cycle before any of the three teams' Q1 commitments are re-planned around it.
