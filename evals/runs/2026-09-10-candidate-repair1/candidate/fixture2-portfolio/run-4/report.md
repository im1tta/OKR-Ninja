## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 9 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The portfolio's worst alignment risk is **AL-11 Circular dependency**: Courier waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, Core Systems waits on Insights' schema validation, and Insights waits on Courier's instrumentation — three committed telemetry KRs with no valid execution order, and C4's pipeline-consolidation bet sits on all three.
No goodness anti-pattern repeats across teams; the largest cluster is calibration and priority hygiene — **AP-05 Everything Is a P0** (Accounts), **AP-07 Unmoored Moonshot** and **AP-08 Committed vs Aspirational Not Labeled** (Courier) — while the only Critical quality defect is **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator** on Dispatch's KR DS2.4.
"On-time delivery rate" is defined two incompatible ways, by Dispatch and by Accounts, on the very number C2 promises to customers (AL-08 Terminology collision, with an 91% vs 78% baseline gap behind it).
Accounts' CSAT target is calibrated on a baseline the company's own Q4 review contradicts by 8 points (AL-09 Baseline disagreement), and Core Systems' "Ship with confidence" claims C2 while measuring nothing C2 names (AL-05 Cascade drift).
Recommended first action: a single Courier/Core Systems/Insights sequencing session that breaks the telemetry cycle by naming which team goes first, before any of the three plans mid-quarter work.
