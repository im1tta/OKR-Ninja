## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 9 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The portfolio's worst alignment risk is **AL-11 Circular dependency**: Courier's instrumentation waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, that GA waits on Insights' schema validation, and that validation waits on Courier's instrumentation — three committed KRs with no valid execution order, and company priority C4's telemetry consolidation sits inside the loop.
No goodness anti-pattern repeats — the eight goodness findings are eight distinct AP-IDs across five teams — so the most consequential is **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator** (Dispatch's "failure rate" KR, the portfolio's only Critical quality defect and the one KR here that cannot be honestly scored).
Two teams also report the same-named metric on incompatible definitions (AL-08 Terminology collision, on-time delivery rate: 91% → 95% vs 78% → 85%), and Accounts calibrates a committed CSAT target on a baseline the Q4 review contradicts by 8 points (AL-09 Baseline disagreement).
Dispatch's committed 600 trial starts depends on a portal Accounts labels stretch (AL-12 Commitment asymmetry), while Accounts marks all four of its objectives P0 (AP-05 Everything Is a P0) — so nothing tells Accounts what to drop when the billing migration slips.
Recommended first action: Core Systems convenes Courier and Insights in week 1 to cut one edge of the telemetry cycle and restate the three KRs in the agreed order.
