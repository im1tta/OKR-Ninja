## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed; 2 Critical, 9 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The worst alignment risk is **AL-11 Circular dependency**: Core Systems gates the telemetry SDK GA on Insights, Insights gates schema v3 validation on Courier, and Courier gates its instrumentation on Core Systems' GA — three teams, one cycle, no execution order that works as written.
No single goodness anti-pattern repeats — eight distinct anti-patterns fire once each — so the most consequential is **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator**, the portfolio's only Critical quality defect: Dispatch's "failure rate" KR names no population and can never be honestly scored.
Two more cross-team defects are live now: Dispatch and Accounts publish two different formulas under one metric name (AL-08 Terminology collision), and Dispatch's committed 600 trial starts ride on a portal Accounts labels stretch (AL-12 Commitment asymmetry).
Underneath that, quality is solid: four of five teams grade B, metric sources are named on nearly every KR, and every objective carries an explicit company-priority link.
The weak spots are calibration and steering signal rather than measurement — K3 and K6 sit at 2 for three teams each.
Recommended first action: Core Systems lead convenes Insights and Courier in week 1 to break one edge of the telemetry cycle before all three teams plan against it.
