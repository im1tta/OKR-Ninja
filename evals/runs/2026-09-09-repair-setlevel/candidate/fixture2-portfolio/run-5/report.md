## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict: At risk.** 5 teams reviewed (Dispatch, Courier, Core Systems, Insights, Accounts); 2 Critical, 9 Major, 2 Minor findings.
The portfolio's biggest threat is **AL-11 Circular dependency**: Courier waits on Core Systems' SDK GA, Core Systems waits on Insights' schema-v3 validation, and Insights waits on Courier's instrumentation — a three-team deadlock with no valid execution order as written, sitting on the critical path of the whole C4 telemetry-consolidation programme.
The second Critical is **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator** (Dispatch KR DS2.4), a percentage whose population is never defined, so the KR can never be honestly scored.
No goodness anti-pattern recurs — eight distinct AP-XX modes fire once each across four teams — so the quality problem is breadth, not a single habit; **AP-13 Ambiguous Denominator** is the most consequential of them.
Two teams also report the same metric name, "On-time delivery rate", under incompatible definitions and 13 points apart (AL-08 Terminology collision), and Dispatch's committed 600-trial-start KR rests on a partner portal that Accounts lists as a stretch item (AL-12 Commitment asymmetry).
Accounts' OKRs are individually the strongest in the portfolio but encode no trade-off at all — every objective is P0 (AP-05).
Recommended first action: Core Systems convenes Courier and Insights within one week to break the telemetry cycle by naming which team ships against the code-complete pre-GA SDK.

(line 31), which asserts Dispatch's methodology without mentioning Accounts or the Billing warehouse; no company-level definition exists, so neither page supersedes the other. (c) *baseline-gap routing (AL-09 vs AL-08)* — the 91% vs. 78% gap is fully explained by the definitional split (cancellations sit inside one denominator and outside the other), so this is reported as AL-08, not as a baseline disagreement.
- Inference labels: none — both definitions, both baselines, both targets and the company-priority phrase are quoted verbatim.

- Recommended resolution owner: Accounts lead (Georg B.) and Dispatch lead (Mei L.) jointly, with the C2 owner arbitrating: publish one canonical "on-time delivery rate" definition before the first monthly business review and rename the other measure (e.g. "in-window job completion rate"), so both KRs can coexist.
