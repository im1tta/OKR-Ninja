## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: AL-02 candidate generation runs the surface-lever key alongside metric identity

The AL-02 Conflicting metrics / adversarial incentives detection heuristic in `references/alignment-taxonomy.md` SHALL define candidate generation as running two independent blocking keys, both always run: (1) a **metric-identity key** — the same canonical metric in two teams' KRs with opposing directionality or incompatible targets — and (2) a **surface-lever key** — two teams' KRs touch the same funnel stage, surface, or population, and one KR's stated lever is a friction, quality, risk, or cost control on that surface while the other KR targets a throughput, conversion, volume, or latency metric of that same surface. A pair generated by either key SHALL become a candidate even when the two KRs share no metric name, and the surface-lever key SHALL require only that the lever be stated in the KR's own text — whether the causal path to the other metric is documented affects labeling and severity under the existing evidence rules, not generation. Part 2 Step 3's blocking-key guidance SHALL name both AL-02 keys (AL-09 remains blocked on metric identity alone), so the procedure step and the entry direct the same generation behavior.

#### Scenario: Fixture defect A1 is caught

- **WHEN** the skill audits `examples/sample-portfolio.md`, where Payments KR P2.2 raises step-up verification coverage to 90% of transactions and Growth KR G2.1 raises checkout conversion from 58% to 68%, with no metric name shared between them
- **THEN** the surface-lever key generates the P2.2 ↔ G2.1 pair as an AL-02 candidate and the alignment report contains the corresponding AL-02 finding (answer-key row A1), with both KRs quoted verbatim and the friction mechanism stated per the entry's evidence rules

#### Scenario: Mechanism pair with disjoint metric names is generated

- **WHEN** two teams' KRs share no canonical metric, but one KR's stated lever is a friction/quality/risk/cost control on the same surface whose throughput or conversion the other KR targets
- **THEN** the pair is generated as an AL-02 candidate by the surface-lever key and proceeds to disconfirmation on its own merits, rather than never being compared

### Requirement: AL-02 candidate generation is distinct from disconfirmation and kills are per-candidate

The taxonomy SHALL present AL-02 candidate generation as a mechanical step that completes before disconfirmation, and SHALL state that disconfirming checks kill candidates one at a time: disconfirming one pair (for example, lookalike metric names that resolve to different definitions or populations) SHALL NOT suppress the generation or the individual judgment of other candidates on the same surface, including surface-lever candidates. The AL-02 disconfirming check in Part 2 Step 4 SHALL carry this per-candidate rule.

#### Scenario: Fixture non-defect 4 is killed without suppressing A1

- **WHEN** the skill audits `examples/sample-portfolio.md` and correctly declines to report Payments KR P1.1 ("checkout success rate") against Growth KR G2.1 ("checkout conversion") — different metrics, different populations, same direction (intentional non-defect 4)
- **THEN** that kill does not remove the checkout surface from surface-lever generation, and the AL-02 finding for P2.2 ↔ G2.1 (answer-key row A1) is still produced

### Requirement: The AL-02 surface-lever key carries a matching in-file example

The AL-02 entry in `references/alignment-taxonomy.md` SHALL include an example in the same file illustrating the surface-lever key — a scenario narrative in which metric-identity blocking generates no candidate, plus the heuristic reading that catches the mechanism-level pair, and a lookalike-metric near-miss that is correctly killed without suppressing the mechanism pair — using only the file's fictional Meridian teams.

#### Scenario: Example present and fictional

- **WHEN** a reader opens the AL-02 entry after this change
- **THEN** they find a worked example of a mechanism-level pair invisible to metric-identity blocking being generated by the surface-lever key, alongside a lookalike-metric pair whose disconfirmation does not suppress it, phrased with fictional Meridian teams and no real company data
